Privacy Policy – Tom Tat Phim

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Tom Tat Phim ("the App") is a private tool used by the owner of the "Tóm Tắt Phim" channel to upload the channel's own videos to its TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token that allow the App to upload and publish videos to the connected account.
  • The status of videos the App uploads (for example, whether publishing succeeded).

2. How we use it

Only to upload and publish the account owner's own videos at the scheduled time and to confirm the result. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local configuration file that is not shared or uploaded anywhere. We do not run a public server that stores user data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is only sent to TikTok through its official API.

5. Other users

The App does not collect any information about other TikTok users or viewers.

6. Your choices

You can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services). After that the App can no longer access your account, and you can ask us to delete the stored tokens by contacting us.

7. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at this address.

9. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/thongkezune/tomtatphim-legal/issues
